# Contributing to letitedit

Thanks for wanting to help. **Bug reports, Look ideas, and small bugfix PRs are welcome.** Please open an Issue before
big changes, so we can talk it over first. Accepted patches are tested and folded into the next release, with credit.

## How changes arrive

Each release here is one commit of the source, made from letitedit's private development repositories. So a pull
request isn't merged here directly: we bring an accepted change into the development code, test it with the rest of the
release, and it arrives in the next release's commit, with your name in its notes.

## Good first steps

- **Found a bug?** Open a [bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml): your letitedit version, Windows
  version, which AI you used, and what happened.
- **Have an idea or a Look you'd like?** Open a [feature or Look request](../../issues/new?template=look_request.yml).
- **Want to fix something small?** Open a pull request against `main` with a short note on what it fixes and how you
  checked it.

Please leave private footage and personal details out of Issues and pull requests.

By sending a change, you agree that it is released under the GNU GPL version 3 or later, like the rest of letitedit.
