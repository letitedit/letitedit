LETITEDIT - SOUND EFFECTS LIBRARY
=============================================
Fun reaction sounds to drop under talking in long-form YouTube, Shorts and other social posts.
Everything in here is free for commercial use (monetized YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook),
and none of it needs a credit line. Every file's source and license is listed in
_Licenses\license_log.csv.

Go through the folders and delete anything you don't like. There are deliberately several options
for each sound.

FOLDERS
  01 Crowd Reactions      cheer, whoop, applause, laugh track, awww, gasp, boo, ooh
  02 Cartoon & Comedy     boing, slide whistle, pop, bonk, slap, rimshot, record scratch, splat, wobble
  03 Animals              cow, duck, crow, cat, owl, songbirds (incl. mountain bluebird), dog, rooster, goat...
  04 Dings & Sparkles     bicycle bell, ding, chime, magic twinkle, cash/coins, level up
  05 Voice Exclamations   short voice clips: "wow", "you win", "congratulations", "oh yeah", "uh oh"...
  06 Real World & DIY     horns, engine, drill, hammer, camera shutter, knock on wood, door creak, clicks
  07 Music Stings         short jingles: sax, pizzicato (sneaky/comedy), 8-bit, steel drum, fail stings
  08 Whooshes & Transitions  whoosh, swoosh, zips, risers, glitches
  _Licenses               license_log.csv plus the license text from each source
  _Download Shortlist.html  hand-picked Pixabay and Mixkit sounds for you to download by hand (see below)

FILE NAMES
  category_description_NN.mp3, e.g. cheer_small-group_02.mp3, knock-on-wood_light_03.mp3
  Everything is MP3 (192 kbps, 44.1 kHz), so it works in Shotcut, CapCut and anything else.
  Long recordings are trimmed to the first 30-60 seconds; trim them further in the editor.

SOURCES (all free for commercial use with no credit)
  Kenney.nl            CC0 game-audio packs (impacts, UI dings, voice clips, jingles)
  Wikimedia Commons    public-domain / CC0 recordings only (e.g. US National Park Service
                       Yellowstone sound library birds and animals)
  Freesound.org        "Creative Commons 0" (CC0) sounds only, pulled with your API key
  Pixabay / Mixkit     NOT bulk downloaded: both sites' terms ban scripts and bots. Use
                       "_Download Shortlist.html" to grab the best ones by hand. On Pixabay, skip
                       any sound whose page says it is registered with Content ID.

Never added: Zapsplat, BBC Sound Effects, CapCut library sounds, meme/TV rips, or anything that
needs attribution or is non-commercial only.

If YouTube ever flags a claim on one of these, check its row in license_log.csv for the source link.
CC0 and public-domain sounds can be disputed using that link.
