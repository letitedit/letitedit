# Travel / event mode

A place or an occasion: a trip, a city, a hike, a festival, a wedding, a launch, a race, a match. What it was like
to be there. Shared craft is in letitedit-craft. Tags: `sources.md`. The library has no travel lesson and one event
lesson (about shooting), so much of this is carried over from documentary craft or is general practice [GEN].

## 1. What drives it
- **Pictures and the place's sound drive; talk is occasional.** Log by picture and sound: places (`letitedit_places`
  groups footage by its own GPS), what happens, the people, the best natural sound (`letitedit_hear` finds cheers,
  music, waves), light and time of day; log talk only where there is some. Not: text-first logging of a mostly
  wordless shoot. Check: every source has a picture note; quiet footage is never marked as junk for being quiet.
  [FEP drivers] [ITE ml7w8spLRtI 00:01]
- **Purpose line:** the feeling of the place or the arc of the event ("the calm of a week off-grid", "the nerves, the
  race, the relief"), or the want if the trip had one ("find the best tacos in town"). Check: the critic names the
  feeling from the render. [JH 20:14] [ITE WlS5f8Plyl0 06:14]

## 2. Structure options
| Shape | Choose when | Avoid when | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| The journey in order | the route itself is the story | the order is only an itinerary | [GEN] |
| Grouped by place or theme (food, people, landscapes) | many places, one feeling | the themes overlap | [GEN] |
| A want carried through the trip | the person set out to find or do something | no want was filmed | [JH 20:14] |
| Event: before, during and after, each with its own emotion (nerves, intensity, release) | an occasion with a peak | there is no build-up footage | [ITE WlS5f8Plyl0 06:14] |
| Mood film carried by music (montage-transformation's rules) | mostly beauty and motion | it needs to inform | [GEN] |

Check: the plan names the shape and the feeling each section carries.
- **Travel earns its time:** keep transit (driving, walking, flying) only when it carries information, subtext or a
  needed breath; otherwise cut to the arrival or bridge with one shot. Not: a fixed time cap, since a slow, beautiful
  journey can be the point. Check: each transit stretch has a stated purpose in the plan. [AOTC Dune] [AOTC MI]
- **Hand over between places:** a pictorial bridge (a plane overhead, then the airport; a sign, then the street), a
  sound that arrives early, or a line that points ahead. Check: every change of place has a named reason to move on.
  [ITE hwecWg2IR18 03:02] [FEP bridge]
- **Cover the event chain:** the venue, the people, the preparation, the build-up, the moment, reactions, the ending,
  the aftermath; report missing links to the person as footage gaps. The aftermath is the closing image or a few
  seconds riding the music's end, not a section of its own after the last event lands (that reads as a second
  ending). Check: the plan maps footage to each link or
  marks it missing. [ITE WlS5f8Plyl0 12:58]

## 3. Pace profile
- **Calm films hold:** 3-10 s wordless shots, slow movement and wide spaces are the grammar of a reflective travel
  film; gears 1-2 with a livelier stretch (a market, a crowd) for contrast. Check: adjacent sections differ in gear;
  long holds carry `hold:` markers. [ITE KAO0n3C0kAg 33:22] [ITE gwyneGrX9Jw 25:15]
- **But beauty alone goes flat:** never three or more pretty standalone shots in a row without a change of state, an
  action chain or a line; turn them into small sequences (ordering, eating, reacting). Check: the critic flags any
  stretch where it wonders why we are still here. [ITE bCVeyUFGxi8 05:37] [ITE eXUH3Z5ZntE 06:45]
- **Events follow the emotion:** before at gears 2-3 and rising, during at 4-5 (cut on the action, hard cuts at the
  peaks), after at 1-2 (the release holds on faces). [ITE WlS5f8Plyl0 06:14] [AOTC Dune]
- **Break the habit when the feel asks:** a hype trip edit can run at gear 4 throughout, with one held breath before
  its peak. [GEN]

## 4. Sound
- **Natural sound is the atmosphere:** waves, markets, wind, a crowd, an engine. Layer it (ambience first, effects as
  their sources appear, music later); put the moments that matter (the cheer, the vows, the starting gun) in
  `brief.protected`; let the place sound play in the gaps instead of wall-to-wall music. Check: protected ranges are
  audible over music; music coverage is chosen per section, not 100%. [ITE gwyneGrX9Jw 10:28] [AOTC GNIP]
  [AOTC Porcelain War]
- **Music for the mood, arranged around the sounds:** `letitedit_soundtrack` builds the track around hits and
  protected ranges (letitedit-sound). [GEN]
- **Replace wind and handling noise** on clips without needed sync with clean ambience from the same place.
  [ITE gwyneGrX9Jw 09:46]

## 5. Text and graphics
- **Orient the viewer:** a place name at the first arrival (a small label or card), a route map (`letitedit_map`, drawn
  from the clips' own GPS or from coordinates) when the route matters, day or date cards for a multi-day trip. Not:
  labels on every shot; a mood film can stay free of text. Check: the critic can say where we are at each new place.
  [GEN]
- **Event text:** the names of the people who matter, and the event's name and date once. [AOTC GNIP] [GEN]
- **Captions only where there is speech** (letitedit-captions); a Reel may need them throughout. [JH 29:50]

## 6. Shot choice and b-roll
- **Establish, then go in:** wides of the place with a small figure for scale or solitude, revealing pans, then mid
  shots of activity and details (textures, food, hands); bookend with walking into and away from camera. Check: each
  new place opens with an orienting shot, or deliberately withholds one. [ITE xZnHgyeZlsU 11:06]
  [ITE xZnHgyeZlsU 18:57]
- **Mood b-roll is legitimate:** a shot that sets the atmosphere of a new place is one of b-roll's jobs, not filler.
  [ITE 9jRZIqPQHm8 07:42]
- **People and reactions** where the moment has one (the first taste, the finish line): they are the emotional proof.
  Check: the event's peak is followed by a reaction when one was filmed. [ITE D2iBdbNej78 04:49]
- **Match cameras** that filmed the same scene (a phone and an action camera) with `letitedit_match_shots`; it leaves
  golden-hour and night looks alone unless told otherwise. [GEN]

## 7. Openings and endings
- **Poetic or urgent:** a slow, layered opening (sound first, long takes) for calm films; the peak moment or the most
  recognisable sight first for hype edits and Shorts. Check: muted, the first 10 s (3 s for a Short) give the feeling
  the plan names. [ITE esNXgCJh-yk 10:52] [JH 10:00]
- **End on a resolved image or phrase:** a closing image riding the end of the music, a walk-away bookend, the
  aftermath or a last look back; not a fade from mid-phrase. Short: end right after the payoff. Check: the music ends
  on a resolving phrase or a cut on the beat. [ITE xhJLcy8Kzl4 16:16] [ITE xZnHgyeZlsU 18:57] [JH 0:42]

## 9. Common failures and how to check
| Failure | Check | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| An itinerary: "and then we went" | the plan has a feeling or a want; sections differ in purpose | [JH 20:14] |
| Postcards: standalone pretty shots with no progression | three or more illustrative shots in a row without a change is a flag | [ITE bCVeyUFGxi8 05:37] |
| Transit padding | each transit stretch has a purpose | [AOTC Dune] |
| Music on top of everything | protected sounds audible; music coverage below 100% by choice | [AOTC GNIP] |
| A lost viewer | a place cue at each new location | [GEN] |
| The event's moment or its reaction missing | the chain map; gaps reported | [ITE WlS5f8Plyl0 12:58] |
| One tempo throughout | adjacent sections differ in gear | [ITE bCVeyUFGxi8 16:38] |
