## External specs (not in the library)
- [RES] EBU R128: programme loudness -23 LUFS (+-0.5 LU for most programmes), maximum true peak -1 dBTP.
  https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128
- [RES] Apple Podcasts audio requirements: -16 LKFS +-1 dB, true peak at most -1 dBFS.
  https://podcasters.apple.com/support/893-audio-requirements
- [GEN] Web -14 LUFS: YouTube turns loud videos down for playback but publishes no reference level; -14 is the commonly
  measured figure and the product default. `letitedit_platforms` marks it unconfirmed for YouTube and Shorts and "not
  documented" for TikTok and Reels (checked 2026-10-19).

## Implementation limits [OURS]
- Score zones outrank the speech dip; speech detection uses voice activity and excludes music tracks.
- Running score after soundtrack replaces its envelope. Stretching changes pitch.
- The soundtrack defaults are merge_gap 4 s, attack 0.3 s and release 0.9 s.
- Check each music track's licence and Content ID status before delivery.
- Dynamic loudness normalization can pump when raw peaks are hot; inspect and listen to the result.
- Local practice supports the actionable rules in the skill, not a general audience-validation claim.
