# Real pictures of named things: fetching, choosing, making, credits (letitedit-graphics section 3)

Read in full before the first `letitedit_real_picture` fetch in a video. The decision to use one, and the order for a board,
are in the skill's section 3.

- **Fetch through the tool, never your own web tools.** `letitedit_real_picture` runs inside letitedit, so nothing asks
  the person for permission; your own web fetch or a shell download can stop an unattended run on a prompt. If you
  found the right page or picture with your own search, give its address to `fetch(url=...)` [OURS].
- **Which candidate:** the thing itself, current, clear at feed size, the plainest background. A logo: the current
  official mark (Commons usually has it as SVG, drawn to PNG for you), not a screenshot of an app. A person: a clear,
  front-on portrait. A book: its cover (Open Library). Look at the preview before you place it [OURS].
- **Making a graphic from it:** use the picture as it is, or make a sticker of it (a cut-out of the thing with a white
  or kit-colour outline and a soft shadow: `letitedit_object` or a `custom` graphic with the picture as `image`), a
  rounded card, or a frame. Keep a logo's shape, colours and proportions as they are: no stretching, recolouring or
  redrawing; trademarks belong to their owners and a distorted mark reads as a fake. A person's photo stays a photo:
  no AI changes to a real person's face [GEN; OURS].
- **Facts to pass on:** each picture keeps a record (source page, licence as stated, author, restrictions such as
  "trademarked"). The export and the review list a credit line for every real picture used; tell the person, who
  decides what to publish. Whatever letitedit's tools make from a real picture (a cut-out or a picture drawn from it,
  a motion clip, a graphic or code scene that shows it) keeps that line, once. A file you make yourself
  from one (your own script or another program) needs `letitedit_real_picture` `made_from` (path, from_files) or its line
  is missed. A licence marked non-commercial (NC) or non-free is a fact to say plainly when you hand the
  video over. Never imply a person or brand shown endorses anything [OURS].
- **Offline or nothing found:** say so in one line and carry on with type, an icon from the asset library or a drawing;
  don't stop the edit for it [OURS].
