# Fonts beyond the bundled ones (letitedit-graphics section 3)

Read before searching for, matching or downloading a font family.

**Fonts beyond the bundled ones.** `letitedit_fonts` searches every Google Fonts family (about 1,950) as well as what's
on this PC: by mood words, by measured shape (`features`: width, heaviness, serif, rounded, contrast, x_height, script,
weight), by `like` (nearest in shape to a family you name), and by a picture: a crop of real text (one line, tight) with
`text` set to exactly what it says ranks every family against it and guesses the weight. Its ranking is a shortlist,
not an answer: look at the sheet and pick by eye; plain sans-serifs have many near-twins, so the first match may be a
look-alike, which is usually fine. A family marked `google` comes down with `get` (or when an edit first names it),
once, with its licence, and then works in exports and the editor's preview [OURS].
