## Tools
- `annotate_tools.py` (kinds, target mapping,
  tracking and loss rules, freeze as an overlay, style keys, refusals, plan, apply and refresh),
  `graphics.py` (custom drawing helpers), `edit_effects.py` (zoom focus in frame
  fractions; keyframes in seconds from the clip's start), `edit_script.py` (transitions, colour items), GUIDE.md
  (items, effects, transitions), `letitedit_review_bundle` project checks (graphics over
  captions) and `letitedit_annotate` previews (labels against faces and other graphics).

## Our own evidence [OURS]
- Local practice: use the actionable rules in the skill; no general audience-validation claim.
- For a music-led montage, chapter captions can carry structure better than transcribing incidental talk.

## General practice [GEN]
Zoom limits, reading times, lower-third length, feed-size widths (360 px for a phone held upright, 480 for wide),
one pointer at a time, one typeface family: common motion-graphics and creator practice, unsourced.
