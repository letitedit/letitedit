# The measured Look's check before the review rounds

Read this in full with a measured Look (its card lists `measured` files: letitedit-learned-look), before the
first review round of letitedit-firstcut section 4. A library Look skips it.

**FIRST-5b The measured Look first.** Do: with a measured Look (letitedit-learned-look), export the fine cut (a preview is
enough) and run `letitedit_look_check(project, video=<that export>)` before the first review round. Fix what it measures on
the whole video: `pace` (stretches, long holds, cuts a minute), `camera`, `grade_likeness` and `sound_shape`, then check
again on the new export. These are measured against the reference by the check itself, so a critic's round never has to
find them, and the two review rounds go on what only a critic sees. A finding the footage can't answer is named as its
limit with what was tried. Not: starting the review rounds with a measured Look finding still open that the footage
could answer. Check: the Look check on the export the first round reviews has no pace, grade-likeness or sound-shape
finding, or each left is named with what was tried. [OURS]
