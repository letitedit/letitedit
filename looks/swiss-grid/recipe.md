# Theme: Swiss grid

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
International Typographic Style in motion: flat paper, black type and bars, one light grey and one signal colour, everything locked to a grid and moving like a printing press.

## The marks
- A grid of a few wide columns with fixed margins and gutters; every bar, block and line of type starts on it.
- Extreme scale contrast: a giant numeral or word next to small technical notes in one sans face (Helvetica or Arial), flush left, ragged right.
- Bars and blocks are flat rectangles; a rule line divides the frame; a light grey carries a second voice under the black.
- Pictures are reduced to geometry that obeys the grid; one element breaks the system, and only that element carries the signal colour.
- No gradients, shadows, glow or grain.

## Colour
Paper, ink, one or two greys and one signal colour (red, orange, ultramarine or green), chosen for the topic and never joined by a second.

## Movement
Everything snaps along the grid and lands on a beat with a short, firm ease; nothing bounces, spins or flies. Rotate, hold, then label.

## What it suits
Explainers, numbers, design and tech, rules and steps, Big Letters Behind You.

## Watch-outs
Too many thin columns look like a chart: use fewer, wider modules. Black on black merges: leave a paper gap between overlapping shapes. Measure text after setting its font and spacing. Keep the giant type clear of the person's head; it may sit behind them only where they don't cover the words that matter.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
