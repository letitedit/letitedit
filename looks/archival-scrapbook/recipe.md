# Look: Archival scrapbook (made with code, no footage; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it on a page from an old field album. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything. (Filmed scrapbook is the other scrapbook Look: it works from the person's own pictures. This one works from an idea alone and has no pictures of theirs in it.)

## What this medium brings
Rag paper gone to parchment, torn cards and tape laid on it, a typewriter's line, cut letters and a red pen, and pictures drawn as copperplate lines.

**How it moves.** Pieces are held up with a far shadow and pressed down; tape goes on after its card lands; the typed line is struck letter by letter; the pen travels. A camera can drift over one large page.

**Where your care goes.** Paper never has a drawn outline: it is torn and throws a soft shadow. Put your care in the drawn pictures on the cards, since there are no photographs, and in the three hands of words (typewriter, cut letters, red pen). Colour is rare and earned.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: torn edges, a parchment tooth over the whole frame, a calm pace of laying and pressing. Faults: bare paper filling the view; a drawing passing for a photograph or a record of what happened; an invented date, name or source on a prop in a true story; words too small to read.

The kit is `M.look.scrapbook`.
