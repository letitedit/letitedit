# Theme: Hand embroidery

The drawing kit is `M.look.embroidery`. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade.

## What it is
Hand embroidery: stranded cotton floss stitched through plain-weave linen in a beech hoop, every mark a length of twisted thread, made stitch by stitch with the needle in view.

## What it suits
Craft, cosy subjects, family, loops.

## Movement
Stitched in, stitch by stitch; stitches can be re-posed in a loop.

## Evidence and watch-outs
Use prepared playback rather than promising smooth live drawing. Fine cloth shimmer varies with picture size; coarser linen pitch has not been tested on a phone. Use a prepared render and inspect the actual export.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; complex marks can hitch. Headless drawing speed does not establish smooth live playback. Check the live player and prepared export at delivery size.

The preview is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
