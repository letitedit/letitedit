# Look: Flat vector (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in flat vector. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
Crisp filled paths with no lines, atmospheric planes, long shadows and a grain brush on the shade side. Flat that still feels like a picture.

**How it moves.** Paths draw on, and plates move under a camera, so it can carry a lot of place and quick camera moves.

**Where your care goes.** The AI's own unprompted scenes are often smooth vector, so what sets this apart is the far planes, the low long shadows and the grain. Without them it reads as any AI video.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: clean shapes with no outlines; plain-colored people; grain only on the shade side. Faults: places that are a few plain shapes on a flat color: clip art with grain on it.

The kit is `M.look.flatVector`.
