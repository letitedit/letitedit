# One clear answer

Style: Interview / podcast. The person, not the decoration, carries one complete thought. This is an answer-led edit, not an automatically shortened conversation or a second-camera simulation.

## Evidence and confidence

- **Medium - professional teaching:** Inside the Edit, [How to Edit Unscripted Dialogue: 3 Stages](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iAKUVUD7fG0), especially the themed selection, reduction and ending examples at 04:57, 07:19 and 08:49. Its usable principle is selecting a thought before decorating a timeline, not a universal duration formula.
- **Medium - professional teaching:** Inside the Edit, [The Biggest Dialogue Editing Mistake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xiJmwycUxTY), 07:40-09:05: the sound of breaths and delivery matters beyond transcript words. These are practitioner demonstrations, not controlled retention studies.
- **Strong for ethical responsibility, not audience performance:** [DAWG's Core Values](https://www.docaccountability.org/values), values 2, 4 and 6: transparency, participant agency and care for audiences. An edit must not give a participant an answer or endorsement they did not give.
- **Low - design proposals to test:** short phrase captions, a modest detail crop on a genuine change of thought, and a moment of room tone at the end. No evidence here establishes one ideal typeface, cut interval or percentage zoom.

## What to select

Read the brief and load the interview/podcast mode alongside the craft and current editing-stage skills. Establish who speaks, what question the passage actually answers, language, permissions and a source-time transcript. Check uncertain words against the sound; a fluent machine translation is not language verification. If meaning remains unknown, stop dialogue selection and say what needs checking. Silence is not missing words to invent.

Find an answer a stranger can understand: a clear proposition, the speaker's reason or example, and their own resolving line. It may be one uninterrupted passage. Keep enough of the actual question or a faithfully paraphrased question label when otherwise the answer is misleading. A hook can be the speaker's most specific line, but must not remove a condition, negation, uncertainty or attribution. Do not assemble new sentences from different answers. Prefer one clean ending to several repeated conclusions. Length follows the complete thought and requested deliverable, not a fixed Shorts quota.

## Picture, words and sound options

- Keep the speaker present for the important claim and its qualification. A single camera may remain a single camera. Never use another interviewee's silent portrait as a listening reaction, or put someone else's speech over visible speaking lips.
- A gentle change of framing can help mark a new idea if the source holds the eyes, head and hands at that crop. It is not a mandatory pulse. Contain a wide shot when vertical cropping would lose essential context; real matching coverage can replace a crop when available.
- Captions can be calm, high-contrast, short sense groups, usually one readable line on a phone; two lines are an option when a name or meaning needs them. Use enough weight and size for the phone, not thin subtitles that only read on a desktop. Keep names, numbers and phrases together, clear of the face, existing source lower-thirds and platform controls. Match the actual words and inspect their export timing. Access captions are not invented quotes or a new narrator.
- Check reading speed as well as line fit. Extend into available space or regroup a complete sense unit rather than making ever smaller chunks. About 20 characters per second is a starting review target, not a universal accessibility standard. Review after changing captions.
- An accurate name/role is optional, introduced once without competing with captions. Omit unverified identity. A persistent logo, progress bar, emoji or diagram earns its place only if requested and meaningful; no default decorative sequence.
- Preserve speech rhythm, breaths that complete thoughts, and natural room tone. Repair intelligibility gently rather than erasing every pause. Music may be absent; if chosen, support the voice without implying suspense, triumph or ridicule the answer does not contain. Respect New video's Music choice.

## Check the actual export

Watch the whole selected answer against its source, including a little context on each side. Check captions, beginning and ending consonants, and that the crop does not drift off the face. Inspect at 1 fps as well as moving picture, measure the mix, and run the review bundle. Record language uncertainty, source restrictions and any part not listened to. No second-camera or two-person proof may be claimed from one speaker. A participant test clip is not automatically cleared for a public product card.
