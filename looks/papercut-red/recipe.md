# Theme: Red paper-cut

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
Window-flower paper-cuts: opaque red paper cut with scissors and laid flat on a paper ground. There is no painting, no gradient and no outline: every detail is a hole.

## The marks
- Each piece on its own canvas: fill the shape, cut the holes with `destination-out`, add paper grain inside the paper only, then lay it down with a tight soft shadow (2-3 px) on the sheet below.
- Cut edges have a slight hand wobble; buildings and machines use straight cuts with the same wobble.
- The cutting vocabulary: rows of crescents (waves, scales, roof tiles), sawtooth edges (grass, fur, fringe), swirls at joints, cloud scrolls, plum-blossom holes, rows of coins, tapered slits.
- Figures and big shapes get an inset contour, a thin slit a little inside the outline: the classic double line, which also separates red over red.
- Suns and flowers are fold-symmetric rosettes: design one wedge, turn it six or eight times.
- No floating islands: a closed ring would drop its centre, so eyes are two crescents with bridges.

## Colour
China red is the hero. The ground and the hero must be different families: by day a light paper ground with pale-red far layers; at night a dark dyed ground with only the important pieces in red. Gold foil for one late moment only.

## Movement
Pieces step at 12 frames a second; a piece is laid down, a fold opens with a snap, a pattern is revealed along a scissor path. Light and camera move smoothly.

## What it suits
Folk tales, celebrations, New Year, food and family stories, anything warm and festive.

## Watch-outs
Red on red becomes one blob: change the ground family, step the paper tones, use inset contours. Tiny holes vanish at phone size: keep the main cuts large. Treat it as the folk craft it is: patterns drawn in code, no copied motif sheets.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
