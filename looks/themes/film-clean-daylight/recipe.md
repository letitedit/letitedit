# Clean daylight

Open, slightly cool surroundings and clear, neutral skin. An uncluttered treatment for an idea that should be easy to follow.

- **Light and grade:** lift the room gently and cool it a little through the inverted face window, and leave the person nearly neutral. Preserve the window's original highlight detail; never lift already-clipped white harder. Reduce a colour cast rather than bleaching skin or whitening eyes.
- **Starting settings (the Theme's picture uses these; adjust to the footage):** lighting `{face_ev: 0.03, face_gamma: 1.05, room_ev: 0}` (the face lift only; the lighting op only darkens); the room's lift on the inverted face-following room oval as Color Grading: lift r/g/b +0.03/+0.035/+0.05, gamma 1.18/1.24/1.34, gain 1.1/1.2/1.34. A stronger face lift clips skin on bright frames. Check the face after both: skin still reads as skin.
- **Pace and cuts:** remove only pauses that interrupt meaning. Cut on a new thought or completed action; use a clear close-up to prove a step. Avoid extra dissolves, slow-motion and decorative jump cuts.
- **Framing:** stable eye-level medium shot with useful context. Keep eye-line and scale consistent across adjacent takes; a single small punch-in may mark a meaningful emphasis, not every sentence.
- **Music feel:** clean, unobtrusive pulse or no music; avoid sparkly effects competing with explanation. Follow the chosen music source and protect the voice.
- **Captions:** dark backing is still needed over a bright room; neutral Inter or another bundled plain face, phrase groups, no bounce obscuring a word.
- **Texture:** normally none. If the selected Look uses grain, glow or leaks elsewhere, do not add them to this treatment automatically.

**Evidence:** E1 supports managing foreground/background and contrast; E2 supports information-led cut-ins. The cool room, restrained pulse and stable medium frame are creative starting points, not proof of trustworthiness. Apply shared limits in film-treatment-notes.md.
