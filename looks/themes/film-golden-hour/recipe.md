# Golden hour

Soft amber highlights and a gently warmer room, with recognisable skin rather than an orange wash.

- **Light and grade:** warm midtones and highlights a little; lower excessive saturation before adding warmth. Put the warmth on the room through the inverted face window; any whole-picture warmth stays small enough that the skin keeps its hue. Keep whites plausible, skin detail intact and bright windows below clipping where the source allows. Do not paint a sun or remove an existing overhead shadow.
- **Starting settings (the Theme's picture uses these; adjust to the footage):** lighting `{face_ev: 0.03, face_gamma: 1.05, room_ev: 0}` (the face lift only); the room's warmth on the inverted face-following room oval as Color Grading: lift r/g/b +0.035/+0.018/0, gamma 1.16/1.06/0.96, gain 1.22/1.06/0.88. A strong blue cut turns blue clothes murky olive; keep blue at or above about 0.88. Check the face after both: skin still reads as skin.
- **Pace and cuts:** carry an action through its completion; let a glance or landscape breathe before the next idea. Use an occasional motivated dissolve across time, not between every speaking angle. Keep the story moving when the performance asks for it.
- **Framing:** preserve the existing direction of light; allow breathing room toward a window or landscape. Keep subject separation, not a compulsory shallow-depth-of-field simulation. Use wider context before a meaningful detail when those shots exist.
- **Music feel:** light acoustic or airy instrumental texture, gently resolving phrases, speech in front. No mandatory cheerful tune and no automatic tempo target.
- **Captions:** neutral white on dark backing; not pale yellow against warm highlights. Keep the face and useful scenery clear.
- **Optional scene texture:** the code-scene filmBurn transition may supply a brief graphic handover only when the edit calls for it. Keep it away from skin and type, use once sparingly, and compare bypass. It is not a glow filter or a new sun; the default treatment needs no leak.

**Evidence:** E1 supports deliberate colour relationships, E2 supports an intentional time-connecting dissolve. Amber, softness and musical palette are creative starting points, not proof of nostalgia or happiness. Apply shared limits in film-treatment-notes.md.
