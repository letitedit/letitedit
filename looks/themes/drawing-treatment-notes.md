# Drawing Theme treatment

The selected Look owns composition, layer order, footage and narration. A kit
Theme supplies the drawing medium described by its individual recipe. Its old
standalone recipe may describe a whole no-footage video; on a talking-head Look
only the illustrated elements take that treatment. It does not replace the
person, source speech, required Look inputs, or the Look's protected text areas.
On Drawn video the kit describes the full illustrated scene. The legacy
kit-recipe-shared.md remains the reference for the kit API and generated scenes;
its no-footage narration assumptions apply only to videos without footage.

Review talking-head composites independently: a standalone illustration does not establish
layering, readable text or preservation of the person in a composite.
