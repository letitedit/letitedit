# Theme: Silkscreen poster

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
A travel-poster screen print being printed: four to six opaque flat inks pulled one after another on cream stock. No gradients, no dots, no line work: an edge is where one ink stops.

## The marks
- Each ink on its own canvas, composited in pull order; later ink covers earlier ink completely.
- Skies and haze are stepped bands: a flat colour, then stripes of the next colour that grow thinner (or thicker) toward the change.
- Bare paper is part of the palette: knock it out of every ink (sails, wave lines, lettering).
- Registration: every ink sits 1-3 px off, so a knocked-out shape leaves a sliver of bare paper; a faint multiplied ghost 2 px off under each ink darkens where inks lap.
- Ink thickness: a light rim top-left and a dark rim bottom-right on fills (never strokes); paper mottle and pinholes show only on ink.
- Mountains and hulls have a lit face and a shade face in two inks; a sun is concentric rings.
- A dark information band at the bottom with big knocked-out lettering and one small line.

## Colour
Few inks, chosen as a set (teal, burnt orange, golden yellow, navy, cream paper). Shadows in the nearest ink, not a new colour.

## Movement
Steady, linear squeegee pulls (a squeegee does not ease), one ink at a time; information prints left to right on the beat. Nothing fades.

## What it suits
Places, travel, events, outdoors, a series of facts about somewhere.

## Watch-outs
Too many inks or any gradient turns it into flat vector art. Stroked edges draw unwanted inner lines on joined shapes: fill only. The info band must stay clear of the platform's covered areas and of the person.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
