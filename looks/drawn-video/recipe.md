# Look: Drawn video

A video made from an idea or supplied script, without recorded footage. The
subject and supplied facts define what is shown. Illustrations carry the story
or explanation; the optional Theme names their medium. With no Theme the medium
is left to the AI. An explanation can use diagrams; a story can use characters
and places. They are one Look rather than separate cards for each drawing kit.

## Inputs and output

The about field is required. A supplied voiceover remains the narration; without
one the available local voice can read a script. Reference pictures remain
references unless explicitly supplied as pictures to show. True stories do not
gain invented events or claims. Projects and generated assets remain editable;
code scenes have a time-driven frame function and render with the export engine.

## Theme boundary

The kit's recipe and themes/drawing-treatment-notes.md describe the chosen
surface. The old drawn-story and flat-explainer recipes remain readable by their
stable IDs; this consolidated Look does not change old projects. A single preview does not validate every Theme combination.
