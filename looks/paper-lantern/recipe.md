# Theme: Paper lantern

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
A shallow box of cut paper sheets lit from behind, like a paper-cut shadow-box lamp. Each sheet is flat and parallel to the front; depth comes from how many sheets there are and how their brightness steps down toward the viewer.

## The marks
- Sheets: each sheet is one flat colour drawn on its own canvas; holes are cut with `destination-out` (set an opaque fill first, or the hole is only half cut).
- Finish on every sheet: fibre grain inside the paper, a warm rim of light along its top edges and a dark band along its bottom edges (the sheet's own shape minus itself shifted a few pixels), and warmth where it is near the lamp.
- Shadows: every sheet casts a soft shadow onto the sheets behind it; never leave them out, they are what makes it paper in a box.
- Light: the back sheet glows; windows, moons, lanterns and wave slits are holes that burn bright, with a little bloom.
- The box: a dark frame around the picture and a gentle vignette inside it.

## Colour
Back sheets pale and glowing, front sheets almost black; that ladder of values is the depth, so never put a pale sheet in front. One temperature per stack (warm dusk, cool night, green forest), warm light, and one accent colour for the thing that matters.

## Movement
Like paper on a stick: sheets slide, turn about a pin or rise into place; the light comes on first, then each sheet's glow in a short stagger, then the windows; a slow flicker and a slow push-in that parallaxes the sheets. No bending or morphing.

## What it suits
Stories, places at night, festivals, memories, calm explainers about a place.

## Watch-outs
Soft shadows can halo: keep blur moderate and offsets downward. A sheet larger than the box shows past the frame. Thin cut strips vanish at phone size: keep holes and bridges chunky. The box frame and vignette belong to the drawing, never over the person.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
