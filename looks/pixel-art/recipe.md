# Look: Pixel art (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in pixel art. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
Cells laid one at a time on a grid, each exactly one of sixteen palette colors, a staircase for an edge and a dither for a tone.

**How it moves.** Cells arrive one at a time; sprites step on the grid. Anything that moves moves in whole cells.

**Where your care goes.** At this size a character is a handful of cells, so put most of your care in hand-laid sprites and hold to a limited palette. Words need room: lettered pixels are big.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: hard stair-stepped edges, sixteen colors, stepped movement, dither. Faults: blurred or smoothly scaled cells; colors outside the palette; sprites so small the action can't be told; words too small to read.

The kit is `M.look.pixelArt`.
