# Look: Rubber hose (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in rubber hose. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
A 1930s studio cartoon: India ink at one pressure on a clear cel, flat greys on its back, noodle limbs, printed on black-and-white film.

**How it moves.** A boiling ink line, and limbs that swing on a beat. Things move in clear poses with a real anticipation before the big move.

**Where your care goes.** The kit gives the gloves, shoes, eyes and the film; the character and the timing are yours. A clear pose on each beat is what sells it.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: black and white; film flicker and dust; bending limbs; a line that never holds still. Faults: a character floating with no pose on the beat; color creeping in; a film look so heavy the subject can't be read.

The kit is `M.look.rubberHose`.
