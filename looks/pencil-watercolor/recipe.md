# Look: Pencil & watercolor (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in pencil & watercolor. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
Graphite and pan washes on cold-press paper: paint that misses its outline, and the sheet showing through.

**How it moves.** A pencil lay-in, then washes spread from where the brush touched. Soft, and slow to arrive, so plan each beat around one wash.

**Where your care goes.** Keep the sheet showing: an evenly covered frame stops reading as watercolor. It's pale by nature, so a subject and any words need their own contrast at phone size.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: soft edges, pale color, washes outside the line, white paper around things. Faults: so pale the subject disappears on a phone; a frame covered edge to edge, so it no longer looks like paint on paper.

The kit is `M.look.pencilWatercolour`.
