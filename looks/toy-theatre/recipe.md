# Look: Toy-theatre paper (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in toy-theatre paper. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
A cardboard stage, painted and printed flats with real gaps between them, and shadows falling between the layers.

**How it moves.** Moved by hand on twos: flats slide, tilt and bob. Depth is the medium, so use several layers.

**Where your care goes.** The stage alone is a template. The things cut out for this video's words are what make it this video, more of them than feels necessary, with painted detail on each.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: movement on twos, which is meant to look stepped; visible card edges and the stage's frame. Faults: a flat with a plain fill; only the theatre with little of the subject in it; no shadow between layers, so it reads as flat stickers.

The kit is `M.look.paperCraft`.
