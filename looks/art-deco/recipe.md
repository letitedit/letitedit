# Theme: Art Deco

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
A 1930s poster that moves: engraved gold line on warm lacquer black, hard-edged volumes shaded like airbrush, and ornament that does a job (a sunburst is the background, a stepped arch is the frame, a dial is the progress bar). Compositions sit on one centre axis.

## The marks
- Ground: warm black (never neutral grey-black), drifting to deep emerald or plum near a horizon.
- The gold line: three strokes on one path: a dark engraved underline a little wider, the gold itself as a banded metal gradient (dark, light, hot, light, dark), and a hairline highlight on top. Move the gradient's offset for a light sweep. Nothing gets a black cartoon outline.
- Volumes: a hard edge filled with a directional gradient from light to shade, one light direction for the whole picture (upper left), a thin gold rim on the lit side.
- Motifs: sunburst wedges, stepped arches and ziggurats, fish-scale rows, chevrons, fans, four-point sparkles, speed lines, dials, a ring of bulbs.
- Type: a geometric display face in ivory or gold, widely spaced, inside a stepped plaque.

## Colour
Three constants (warm black, gold, ivory) and one saturated accent kept for the one thing the story follows (burgundy, coral, jade). A cooler jewel tone may carry a secondary surface, never the same area as the accent.

## Movement
Mechanical and symmetrical: lines engrave themselves from the centre outward, doors split along the axis, bulbs switch on one by one so they can be counted, a sheen crosses gold on a hit. Firm ease-in-out, nothing floats.

## What it suits
Launches, countdowns, awards, events, luxury, hospitality, anything with a deadline.

## Watch-outs
Gold reads as brown when the gradient is too dark or too wide: keep a bright band inside every line. A plain character looks pasted in: draw people with the same airbrush volumes and gold rim. Fish-scale rows read as brickwork unless each row overlaps the one before. Keep small gold text away from the person's face edge.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
