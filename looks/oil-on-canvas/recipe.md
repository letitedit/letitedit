# Look: Oil on canvas (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in oil on canvas. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
Opaque paint with hog-bristle brushes on toned linen, from a lean block-in to a fat impasto, painted dark to light in front of the viewer.

**How it moves.** A painting is a still that is being made: block-in to impasto, stroke by stroke. To move after that, paint its parts as separate works, or drift the camera.

**Where your care goes.** The light is yours: `color(x, y)` decides the picture. Strokes have to follow form, or they read as wood grain; a few hundred is plenty. Turn the strokes with each form: around a round tower, along a roofline, across a door's planks and in small broken dabs on a wall, so no two surfaces share one direction. Parallel streaks all over the frame are the first thing a viewer will read as wood. Every shot opens with its subject already readable: paint the block-in of the thing itself, not a dark field the subject arrives on a second later, and keep to one or two paintings being made in a video, with the others already finished.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: slow arrival of each painting, visible brushwork, loose edges. Faults: strokes that look like wood grain or noise rather than paint; a second or more of a dark field with no subject; a long stretch of a finished painting with nothing changing.

The kit is `M.look.paintedOil`.
