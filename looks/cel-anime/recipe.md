# Theme: 80s cel anime

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
Hand-painted cel animation of the late 1980s, rebuilt in code: an airbrushed background painting, flat cels on top, light glowing through the frame, all played back like a videotape on a CRT.

## The marks
- The background painting, made once: soft gradients for sky (indigo to magenta to orange at dusk), blurred cloud streaks with lit undersides, a glowing sun with a wide halo, hills and buildings with small lit windows, wet water that breaks the light into glints.
- Cels: flat fills, then a hard-edged shadow shape, then a hard highlight shape, then a coloured outline (a dark version of the fill, never black). No gradients inside a cel.
- Light through the frame: lamps, signs and lanterns as small cores with added halos that never bleed through a cel in front.
- Videotape: the red channel shifted a few pixels against green and blue, a soft bloom, scanlines, faint noise, a tracking line, darkened corners.
- People: long faces, almond eyes near the middle of the head, nose as one shadow plane; this is the hardest part, so frame people from behind, in silhouette or small when you can.

## Colour
Plates carry the atmosphere; cels are few and saturated. Each time of day gets its own colour set rather than a filter over everything; the main character keeps one accent colour that pops in every set; skin keeps its own colour.

## Movement
Cels step at 12 frames a second (or 8); the background slides smoothly underneath; light and camera move on every frame.

## What it suits
Stories, nostalgia, music, night cities, travel at dusk.

## Watch-outs
Without characters it can read as generic synthwave: give the cels a real job (a vehicle, a figure, a sign). White cels bloom pink: keep bloom weak on the colour layer. The videotape pass is for the drawing only: it doesn't shift, scan or tint the person above it.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
