# Theme: Broken colour

The drawing kit is `M.look.brokenColour`. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade.

## What it is
Broken colour: plein-air dabs of unmixed oil on primed linen, side by side for the eye to mix, high key and full of light.

## What it suits
Sunrise travel, mood, landscape, painting subjects.

## Movement
Dabs arrive patch by patch.

## Evidence and watch-outs
Broken strokes need tonal structure; colour noise alone does not make a painting. Check the small-card silhouette.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; complex marks can hitch. Headless drawing speed does not establish smooth live playback. Check the live player and prepared export at delivery size.

The preview is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
