# Theme: Stained glass

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
A window of cut coloured glass held in dark lead, seen from inside a dim stone room, glowing only where light comes through it. The window is the picture; the room and its floor are always there.

## The marks
- Pieces: every region is cut into hand-sized pieces (a jittered lozenge lattice, wavy bands for water, wedges for a sun), each with its own small shift of hue and depth and a few faint streaks at its own angle. One colour is one piece.
- Lead: near-black lines between all pieces, thicker round the main shapes, round joins. Iron saddle bars cross the whole window.
- Painted detail: faces, folds, planks and seams are thin translucent brown lines painted on the glass, never a black outline.
- Light: multiply the glass by a light map, brightest behind the sun or the subject; unlit glass stays dark and milky. A blurred, added copy lets bright glass eat into the lead.
- The room: dark warm-grey stone blocks, a lighter splayed reveal around the arch, a sill, large flagstones. The window's colours land on the floor as a mirrored, blurred, softened patch, with a faint shaft and dust.

## Colour
Two colours carry the picture, one deep and one hot (cobalt and ruby), with small touches of gold, green and a greenish white. Keep blue strong; faces darker than you expect, or backlit faces burn white.

## Movement
Glass never bends: things appear by being lit. Light sweeps across the window, panes come up one after another, figures move piece by piece in steps.

## What it suits
Stories, history, legends, turning points, reveals, anything told as chapters.

## Watch-outs
Borrow the craft, not the subjects: no religious figures or symbols unless the video is about them. A perfect Voronoi or flat fills read as a filter or clip art: keep textured, hand-cut pieces. A sharp floor patch looks like a reflection: blur and dim it. Floor light and shafts belong to the drawn room and never fall on the person.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
