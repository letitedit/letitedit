# Look: Crayon (made with code, no footage; not approved; a drawing-kit Look)

Style: made with code. Flavour: a voice tells something while the AI draws it in crayon. The person says what it's about. Every other call is yours, and nobody is asked anything.

## What this medium brings
Wax on toothy paper: back-and-forth scribble fills that skip the valleys, a darker crayon on the shade side, a white one burnishing the light, and a fat wobbly contour gone over twice.

**How it moves.** Scribble fills arrive stroke by stroke, so being seen drawn is the charm of it. Lines can boil across a few takes.

**Where your care goes.** Crayon forgives wobble, not vague shapes. Curved strokes that follow a form, a shade on one consistent side, and many small marks that belong to this video.

**What the review should expect of it.** By design: wobble, paper showing through every stroke, colors that mix where they overlap, bare paper around things. Faults: an even flat fill with a crayon texture on top; shapes so rough the subject can't be told.

The kit is `M.look.crayon`.
