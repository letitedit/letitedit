# Before and after

A real change, made legible through chosen footage rather than a zoom preset.

## Material and structure

Use footage of one identifiable project or object, including an initial state,
consequential work, and a genuinely different result. Log the whole folder before
choosing: neutral filenames and camera order do not describe a story. Discard
repeated coverage from the cut, not from the source folder. A transformation can
be a build, restoration, garden, room or craft; do not imply an unfilmed finish.

The opening can show the before, pose a visible question, or briefly foreshadow
the result. Establish enough of the initial condition to make the final comparison
meaningful. Group the middle by changes that cause the next state. Compress
repeated work but keep the movements which explain how something changed. Release
the pace before the reveal and let the actual after register. A matched angle,
detail callback, split comparison or plain cut can clarify the difference; none
is compulsory. Camera punches alone are not an edited transformation.

## Timing, framing and sound

The scale of the work sets the shot scale: context, hands or mechanism, result.
Portrait needs the action and object together. If cropping hides necessary
geography, use an intentional wide composition and record why. Music can carry
phrases around stages, then change or resolve on the reveal. Preserve useful
source sounds and duck music for meaningful contacts. Never invent location
sound or narration and call it original. Sparse truthful labels are optional;
no fabricated dates, performance claims or completion milestones. Caption speech
only when it exists. Natural-sound-led and music-led versions are both valid.

## Evidence and limits

- [Practitioner synthesis] StudioBinder, Soviet Montage Theory,
  https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/soviet-montage-theory/: contrasting or
  supporting images establish meaning, pace and tone. This is craft advice, not a
  controlled test of this Look or evidence for a numeric cut rate.
- [Practitioner references] The sources cited in
  skills/letitedit-modes/references/montage-transformation.md discuss music-led
  selection, phrase changes and the before/work/after shape. Their agreement is
  not audience validation of any particular sample.
- [Proposed heuristics] Establish object identity, choose causal stages, vary
  density, and give the reveal breathing room. Durations follow the material,
  not a fixed template. Check actual moving joins and listen to the mix.

## Source limits
Keep the final state honest: an assembled component is not the finished deployed product. Public-domain status does not grant endorsement, brand use of insignia, or likeness clearance. Construction coverage does not by itself supply intimate hands-and-tools sound.
