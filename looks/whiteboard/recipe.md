# Theme: Whiteboard

There is no drawing kit for this Theme: draw it with plain Canvas 2D in a code scene (`letitedit_code_scene`), with the motion runtime's helpers. This is a drawing treatment, not a source-footage grade. `scene.js` beside this recipe draws the Theme's grid picture, the same small boat every drawing Theme draws (its shape names and `X`, `Y`, `K` come from that picture's shared subject): read it as a worked example of the marks, not as a layout.

## What it is
An explainer drawn live in dry-erase marker on one glossy whiteboard, written in real stroke order. No hands, no cursor: markers float in and out with their own shadows.

## The marks
- The board: a warm white with faint ghost marks of an older lesson (grey or pale blue, 5-10%) and a soft diagonal sheen that drifts slowly; a thin aluminium frame and tray only when the edge is in view.
- Marker strokes: rounded caps, a slow hand wobble, a little overshoot past every corner (shapes are drawn edge by edge), circles that overlap past their start, a darker pooled dot where the nib lands (kept inside the stroke width), and faint dry streaks along the stroke. Overlaps darken like real ink.
- Lettering: a handwriting face written in, or single-stroke letters drawn in order.
- Props: a floating marker with a soft cast shadow that grows when it lifts, round magnets, an eraser that can wipe.

## Colour
Three marker colours at most, each with one meaning kept for the whole video (dark ink for things, blue for process, one accent for what matters).

## Movement
Strokes are revealed at the speed of a hand (a dash offset along the path); the board is a map, and the view travels from region to region led by the pen. Erasing can rewind an idea.

## What it suits
Explainers, how-it-works, science, plans and diagrams.

## Watch-outs
One pen doing every drawing falls behind the voice: give each drawing a time window. Very thin strokes vanish at phone size: draw at 6-11 px on a 1080-wide frame. Keep the busiest writing away from where the person sits in the frame.

Live drawing needs a graphics-capable PC; full-frame blurs, glows and per-piece textures cost time. Draw what does not change once to its own canvas and move the finished canvas. Check the live player and the prepared export at delivery size.

The grid picture is a generated drawing study, not camera footage or approval of a complete video.

## Behind a person
Change only the illustration and backdrop. Keep the person above the drawing; their picture (its size, grade and lighting) is the Look's, and drawn pieces and effects stay off their face where they can. Keep their recorded voice. See the shared drawing-treatment notes for the layer route.
