# Trailer

A promise of a film, not its whole plot shortened.

## Story and selection

Log the complete source film or shoot: who wants what, the situation, the turn,
the genre and its strongest truthful moments. Build a comprehensible setup before
an obstacle changes its direction. Select across the available material, not
just the first scene. A short escalating sequence can promise variety, followed
by a title and a final button, question or held image. This is one useful shape,
not a required five-card template. Do not reveal a resolution merely because it
is the biggest shot. Do not manufacture dialogue, reactions or relationships.

## Pace, picture and sound

Let performances establish the tone, then vary density around a real turn.
Comedy needs setups and enough time to read the physical gag; a cut on every
beat can destroy it. Suspense may use withholding and a held image instead.
Music and original dialogue or effects can bridge ellipses, gain intensity,
drop for a beat, and resolve or cut deliberately at the button. Use only cleared
recordings. A silent film has no original synchronized production audio to
recover: use a cleared score, never label fabricated effects as source sound.
Titles earn their place by orienting or making the film promise explicit.
Preserve readable intertitles, character identity, eyelines and spatial facts.
Portrait is an option, not an excuse to remove another performer or the action's
cause. Wide staging can require a landscape trailer; document that choice.

## Evidence and limits

- [Practitioner advice] Film Editing Pro, How to Begin Your Trailer Music Editing
  Process, https://www.filmeditingpro.com/how-to-begin-your-trailer-music-editing-process/:
  title anchors and a developing music arc help organize a trailer. This is craft
  advice, not proof of audience response or a prescribed seconds-per-shot figure.
- [Practitioner references] skills/letitedit-modes/references/creative-stylised.md
  and its cited Film Editing Pro trailer discussion support motivated form and
  genre signaling. Comedy timing is material-dependent.
- [Proposed heuristics] Setup, turn, escalation, title and last beat are options
  to judge against clarity and curiosity. A critic must be able to name the
  promise without requiring unshown backstory. Watch every second and the joins;
  a clean technical gate alone cannot establish a good trailer.

## Source and delivery limits

Choose distinct source ranges and preserve staging and source aspect ratio when simultaneous action matters; do not stretch it. Verify any later accompaniment separately from the original film. Public-domain status in one country is not worldwide clearance.
