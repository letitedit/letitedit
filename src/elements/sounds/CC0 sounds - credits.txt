CC0 sound effects for the Elements panel (letitedit)
====================================================

Every sound here is free to use in any video, including commercial and monetised ones, with no credit
line needed. They replace Shotcut's bundled GDC sounds, whose licence forbids AI use.

Sources: Kenney.nl (CC0), Freesound.org (CC0 sounds only) and Wikimedia Commons (public domain / CC0),
from the CC0 library letitedit keeps in assets\sfx\library (license_log.csv lists every original);
and "letitedit" = made by letitedit's own code (tools\assets\synth_sfx.py, no outside recordings),
released under CC0 1.0. Converted to FLAC (the Elements panel lists .flac files).
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
