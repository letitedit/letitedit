letitedit {version}: a video editor for creators that your own AI (Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity) edits with you, built on Shotcut 26.9.27. Free, and free software. This is a beta.

**Download:** `{installer}` below, for 64-bit Windows.

**Windows warns you once.** The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Check the download:** `SHA256SUMS.txt` below lists every file's SHA-256.

**Source code:** the "Source code" archives below are this release's source: the tool server, skills and installer scripts at the top, the editor in `src/`. `{msys2}` holds the source of the MSYS2-built libraries the installer bundles, and `{mlt}` the source of MLT (letitedit's patches to it are in `release/source/mlt-patches/`).
